# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

CAD-EvoLoop contributors must maintain a professional, inclusive, and
evidence-based working environment. Acceptable conduct includes respectful
technical disagreement, clear attribution, responsible disclosure, and care
with licensed or confidential data.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate data or
result manipulation, and publication of private information without permission
are not acceptable.

## Scope and Enforcement

This standard applies in project spaces and when representing the project in
public. Report conduct or research-integrity concerns through the private
security contact described in `SECURITY.md`. Maintainers may remove content,
restrict participation, or refer serious matters to the relevant institution.

Enforcement decisions should document the reported behavior, applicable policy,
and action taken while protecting reporter privacy.
